AI DISRUPTION

Preparing for AI Disruption, Cyberattacks & Cascading Infrastructure Failures

This is not a prediction. It is a practical plan for keeping your household functioning if systems we normally depend on suddenly become unreliable.

The good news: you probably already have a head start.

Most of what LivSolv recommends for blackouts, storms, water interruptions and being stranded away from home also helps here. Backup power, stored water, independent communication, emergency information, food and a good car kit do not care what caused the disruption.

BIOLOGICAL BASELINE

NIOSH-approved N95 + sealed goggles + nitrile gloves

A practical baseline for reducing exposure to some airborne biological particles and protecting eyes and hands. Goggles should actually cover and protect the eyes, not ordinary eyeglasses.

Add simple skin coverage: long pants, a long-sleeve shirt or lightweight jacket, socks and closed shoes. The goal is less exposed skin and clothing you can remove before bringing contamination farther inside.

EXTREME-CASE OPTION

Full-face respiratory protection

For people who want a higher preparedness tier, full-face respiratory protection can combine eye and respiratory protection. The correct respirator, filter or canister depends on the hazard, so this is not a universal shield.

Then reduce the dependencies

Keep some cash in small denominations, more than one payment method, printed family contacts, a family verification word for suspicious calls, stored water, food you can prepare without normal utilities, and important information available offline.

That is the larger LivSolv idea: do not try to predict exactly what fails. Make sure your family still has options when it does.
