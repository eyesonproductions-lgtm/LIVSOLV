COOL + ACTUALLY USEFUL

Emergency gear that earns its space.

No tactical junk drawer. No gadget gets on this page just because it looks clever. These are things that solve a real problem, combine several jobs, or make an emergency noticeably easier.

Some of these are things we personally own. Some are products we researched because they solve a specific problem unusually well. We label the difference clearly. The standard is simple: would we still tell you about it if there were no affiliate link?

You do not need everything here. Start with the problem most likely to leave you stranded, in the dark, disconnected, overheated, or unable to handle a basic repair.

The LivSolv gadget ruleCool is not enough. It has to reduce a problem, replace another piece of gear, or meaningfully increase your options.

The underrated category: fixing things

A lot of emergencies are not solved by a survival gadget. They are solved by being able to patch, strap, seal, cut or improvise.

Gorilla Tape
Fast patching and reinforcement.
Heavy zip ties
Temporary fastening and bundling.
Self-fusing silicone tape
Useful around hoses and leaks.
Hose clamps
Tiny, cheap mechanical insurance.
Super glue
For equipment repair, not wounds.
Small sewing kit
Clothing, packs and fabric repairs.
Paracord
Securing, tying and improvising.
Work gloves + basic hand tools
Because emergencies are hard on hands.
