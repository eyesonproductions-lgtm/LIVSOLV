Important battery safety

Use only rechargeable NiMH or NiCd cells approved for the charger. Never put ordinary alkaline batteries in a charger.

LivSolv rule: do not depend on one battery, one charger or one kind of light. A headlamp, an area lantern and more than one power path give you useful redundancy without filling a closet with gear.
