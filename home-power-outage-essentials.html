Do these free things first

Light: gather every flashlight, headlamp, lantern and spare battery already in the house.

Phones: use Low Power Mode, lower brightness and stop unnecessary streaming or scrolling.

Food: keep refrigerator and freezer doors shut. Group frozen food together.

Water: if the outage may affect a well pump or local water service, fill clean food-safe containers while water is still available.

Temperature: gather blankets and warm clothing in cold weather. In heat, block direct sun and move to a cooled safe location if conditions become dangerous.

Information: write important numbers on paper, save useful information offline and locate any battery radio you already own.

Cooking: choose no-cook food first. Never move outdoor fuel-burning cooking equipment indoors.

Food safety clock

Refrigerator

An unopened refrigerator generally keeps food safely cold for about 4 hours.

Freezer

A full unopened freezer can hold temperature for about 48 hours. A half-full freezer is closer to 24 hours.

Critical generator safety: Never run a portable generator, grill, camp stove or other fuel-burning equipment inside a house, basement, garage or camper. Keep portable generators outside, at least 20 feet from the home and from doors, windows and vents, with exhaust directed away. Use a battery-powered or battery-backup CO alarm inside.
