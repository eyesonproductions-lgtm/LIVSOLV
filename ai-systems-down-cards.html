PAYMENTS DOWN
WHAT TO DO FIRST
LIVSOLVAI SYSTEMS DOWN CARD
DO THIS NOW

Cards, ATMs or banking apps may fail.

  • Check whether cash or card payments work before shopping.
  • Keep any cash you already have safe; small bills help.
  • Write an essentials list: food, medicine, transport.
  • Ask businesses which payment methods they accept.
  • Never share PINs or passwords with emergency callers.

CELL NETWORK DOWN
WHAT TO DO FIRST
LIVSOLVAI SYSTEMS DOWN CARD
DO THIS NOW

Calls and texts may not go through.

  • Try texts briefly; conserve battery.
  • Agree on a household meeting point in advance.
  • Choose an out-of-area contact.
  • Check radio or official in-person notices.
  • For urgent help, try 911; if unavailable, seek safe local help.

POWER GRID DOWN
WHAT TO DO FIRST
LIVSOLVAI SYSTEMS DOWN CARD
DO THIS NOW

Electricity may fail across a large area.

  • Use daylight and flashlights you already own.
  • Keep refrigerator and freezer doors closed.
  • Unplug sensitive devices; conserve phone charge.
  • Check on neighbors needing powered medical devices.
  • Never use grills, generators or camp stoves indoors.

WATER SYSTEM DOWN
WHAT TO DO FIRST
LIVSOLVAI SYSTEMS DOWN CARD
DO THIS NOW

Taps, treatment or sewer systems may fail.

  • If warned ahead, fill clean food-safe containers.
  • Set aside drinking water first; remember pets.
  • Check utility boil-water or do-not-drink advisories.
  • Use less water; avoid unnecessary flushing.
  • Boiling kills germs, not chemical contamination.
FREE: Store whatever clean water you can; any amount helps.

FOOD SUPPLY DOWN
WHAT TO DO FIRST
LIVSOLVAI SYSTEMS DOWN CARD
DO THIS NOW

Stores and deliveries may be disrupted.

  • List food already in your pantry and freezer.
  • Plan meals around foods you actually eat.
  • Add one shelf-stable item only when affordable.
  • Ask food pantries about support before a crisis.
  • Keep cold food cold; never taste-test spoiled food.

TRANSPORT DOWN
WHAT TO DO FIRST
LIVSOLVAI SYSTEMS DOWN CARD
DO THIS NOW

Traffic signals, transit and logistics may fail.

  • Avoid unnecessary travel during widespread disruptions.
  • Use offline or paper maps for alternate routes.
  • Treat dark traffic signals as all-way stops unless directed.
  • Know safe walking routes and a meeting place.
  • Never drive into floodwater or around road closures.

WHAT TO DO FIRST

DO THIS NOW

Pharmacies, records and appointments may be disrupted.

  • Write down medication names, doses and allergies.
  • Keep clinician and pharmacy numbers on paper.
  • Refill essential prescriptions on schedule.
  • Ask a pharmacist about emergency refill options.
  • For immediate danger, seek emergency care.

MULTIPLE SYSTEMS DOWN
WHAT TO DO FIRST
LIVSOLVAI SYSTEMS DOWN CARD
DO THIS NOW

Several services may fail at the same time.

  • Check immediate safety: shelter, water, medical needs.
  • Account for household members; agree on meeting point.
  • Inventory food, water, medication and usable power.
  • Use trusted official updates; verify alarming claims.
  • Help neighbors where safe; reassess staying or leaving.
