# LivSolv Emergency Home Binder

**Working product concept**

A calm, practical, fill-in home preparedness system. Not an apocalypse planner and not a giant generic checklist. The binder should let a household answer: **What do we have, where is it, what do we do first, and what are we missing?**

## Product strategy

- Printable PDF sold directly by LivSolv.
- Later physical binder / workbook edition if demand supports it.
- Companion to the LivSolv book and website, not a copy of either.
- Website stays current for product recommendations and affiliate links.
- Binder uses short LivSolv URLs / QR destinations for changing gear recommendations rather than hard-coding products that will become obsolete.
- Create a free mini version later as an email-list lead magnet, while the full binder remains paid.

## Binder architecture

### 1. START HERE: My household in one page
- Household members
- Children / older adults / people needing assistance
- Pets
- Important medical considerations
- Primary emergency contacts
- Out-of-area contact
- Home address + GPS/location notes if useful
- Primary meeting place
- Backup meeting place
- Evacuation destination

### 2. Emergency action cards
Designed to be usable under stress, not read like chapters.
- Power outage
- Water outage / boil advisory
- Severe storm / tornado
- Flood
- Extreme heat
- Extreme cold
- Wildfire / evacuation
- House fire
- Medical emergency
- Communications outage

Each card: **DO FIRST / THEN / LEAVE IF / WHERE THE SUPPLIES ARE**.

### 3. Water
- People + pets daily water calculation
- Water currently stored
- Container inventory and locations
- Fill-before-storm checklist
- Water treatment methods owned
- Boiling capability
- Backup natural water sources nearby, clearly marked as UNTREATED
- Reminder: improvised seep wells and natural water still require appropriate treatment
- Water rotation log
- LivSolv current water gear QR/link

### 4. Food
- 72-hour no-cook meals
- 7-day food plan
- 14-day pantry inventory
- Foods requiring no heat
- Foods requiring minimal heat/water
- Special dietary needs
- Manual can opener location
- Cooler inventory
- Freezer plan
- Food rotation / expiration tracker

### 5. Blackout cooking + fuel
- Primary cooking method
- Backup cooking method
- Indoor-safe vs outdoor-only designation
- Fuel type
- Fuel quantity
- Fuel storage location
- Ignition backups
- Pot/kettle for boiling water
- Approximate meals/boils the household can support
- Bad-weather cooking plan
- No-cook fallback
- CO alarm locations + battery check
- LivSolv current emergency cooking gear QR/link

### 6. Power
- What absolutely must stay powered
- Device wattage / runtime worksheet
- Power bank inventory
- Power station inventory
- Solar panel inventory
- Generator inventory
- Fuel inventory
- Charging cables / adapters map
- Refrigerator/freezer strategy
- Medical-device priority section
- Battery maintenance / recharge dates
- LivSolv current backup-power recommendations QR/link

### 7. Heat + cold
- Warm-room choice
- Cool-zone choice
- Doorway/window barriers owned
- Sleeping bags / blankets / pads
- Fans / cooling equipment
- Safe heating equipment
- Tent-inside-house option
- CO/smoke alarm check
- When-we-leave thresholds

### 8. Communications
- Family phone numbers on paper
- Out-of-area contact
- Meeting locations
- NOAA radio frequency / local stations
- FRS/GMRS radio channels used by family if applicable
- Satellite communicator information
- Charging plan
- Paper map location
- Neighbor/support network

### 9. Medical
- First-aid kit location
- Backup kit location
- Medication list
- Allergies
- Physicians / pharmacy
- Insurance information
- Medication refill / reserve dates
- First-aid training dates
- AED locations nearby if known
- Household-specific medical equipment
- Emergency information cards
- LivSolv current first-aid kit + add-on recommendations QR/link

**Boundary:** general preparedness organization only. The binder must not prescribe treatment or encourage care beyond training.

### 10. Vehicle
- Vehicle emergency kit inventory
- Jumper / cables
- Tire equipment
- Water / food
- Seasonal gear
- Maps
- Fuel rule (for example, household-selected minimum tank level)
- Maintenance dates
- Evacuation loading plan

### 11. Evacuation / go now
- 5-minute list
- 15-minute list
- 60-minute list
- Go-bag locations
- Documents to grab
- Medications
- Pet carriers/leashes
- Vehicle assignment
- Two routes out
- Destination A / B / C
- Family reunification plan

### 12. Home systems
- Main water shutoff location + photo space
- Electrical panel location + photo space
- Gas shutoff information (only actions consistent with utility guidance)
- Fire extinguisher locations
- Smoke/CO alarm map
- Generator connection information
- Septic/well notes
- Solar/battery system notes
- HVAC information
- Important contractor / utility numbers

### 13. Documents
- What documents exist
- Where originals are stored
- Where encrypted/off-site backups are stored
- IDs/passports
- Insurance policies
- Home inventory
- Vehicle titles/registration
- Pet records
- Key account/contact information

**Security rule:** do not design pages that encourage users to print passwords, full card numbers, SSNs, or other unnecessary secrets into an easily stolen binder.

### 14. Money when systems are down
- Emergency cash denomination plan
- Cash location field with privacy warning
- Bank/contact information without sensitive login credentials
- Household emergency spending priorities

### 15. Pets
- Food/water quantities
- Medications
- Vet
- Carriers
- Leashes/harnesses
- Vaccination/medical records
- Evacuation destination that accepts pets
- Comfort/containment supplies

### 16. Skills + drills
- First aid/CPR
- Fire extinguisher use
- Water shutoff
- Power/generator procedure
- Family communication drill
- Evacuation drill
- Equipment practice
- Last practiced / next practice

### 17. Inventory + gap analysis
For every capability:
- HAVE IT
- KNOW WHERE IT IS
- KNOW HOW TO USE IT
- NEED TO REPLACE/BUY
- $0 ALTERNATIVE
- PRIORITY

### 18. Maintenance calendar
Monthly / seasonal / annual:
- Recharge batteries
- Rotate water
- Rotate food
- Test smoke/CO alarms
- Check extinguishers
- Run/test generator as manufacturer requires
- Inspect first-aid supplies
- Update medications
- Update contacts
- Update documents
- Seasonal vehicle kit swap
- Family drill

## Commercial design principle
The binder should never become a catalog. Gear pages point to stable LivSolv topic URLs where recommendations can change over time. This preserves the usefulness of a printed binder and lets LivSolv earn affiliate revenue when a reader later needs equipment.

## Possible paid versions
1. **Emergency Home Binder - Digital**: printable/fillable PDF.
2. **Emergency Home Binder - Family Edition**: expanded household, child, pet and reunification planning.
3. **Emergency Home Binder - Physical Workbook**: print-on-demand or fulfillment only after demand is demonstrated.

## Companion book concept
Working direction: **LivSolv: The Practical Guide to Getting Through Almost Anything**.

The book teaches *why and how*. The binder organizes *your household's actual plan*. LivSolv.com maintains *current products, comparisons, updates and deeper guides*.
