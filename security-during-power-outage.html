Home + Family · Outage security

Keep watch when the power and Wi-Fi are down.

Build inexpensive layers that use sunlight, batteries and cellular service instead of depending on the same grid and router that may fail.

The LivSolv approach: light the approaches, create local alarms at doors and windows, add early warning farther from the house, and use a cellular solar camera where seeing what happened actually matters.

Build four simple security rings

Ring 1 · Property edge: driveway alert and cellular camera at the road, gate or long approach.

Ring 2 · House perimeter: solar motion lights covering dark sides, rear approaches, garages and yards.

Ring 3 · Entry points: solar lighting at doors and steps plus battery door/window alarms.

Ring 4 · High-value equipment: generator, fuel, solar equipment, shed, detached garage or RV gets its own light, lock and camera where appropriate.

6. Physical security still works with zero watts

Doors: test deadbolts and inspect frames and strike plates.

Windows: repair failed locks; appropriate stops can add a physical barrier on compatible sliding windows and doors.

Garage: know how the manual release works and how the door can be secured when the opener is unavailable.

Generators and fuel: keep generators outdoors in a safe carbon-monoxide location while securing valuable equipment against easy removal without creating a ventilation, fire or trip hazard.

Gates, sheds and RVs: inspect locks before storm season.

Lowest-cost setup: HMCITY or Aootek solar motion-light multipack + inexpensive battery door/window alarms.

Better setup: add path lighting and a driveway alert.

Best-value monitored setup: add one SPYPOINT FLEX-M Solar Bundle at the most important approach.

Premium setup: use brighter perimeter floodlights and a Tactacam REVEAL Pro 4.0 Solar where more advanced remote monitoring is worth the additional cost.

What should I do before a storm?

Charge rechargeable devices, confirm camera plans are active, clean solar panels, replace weak alarm batteries, walk-test motion zones after dark and verify local camera storage where supported.

No bunker required. A few independent, affordable layers are more resilient than one expensive security system with a single point of failure.
