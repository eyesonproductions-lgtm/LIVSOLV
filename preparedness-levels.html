How prepared do you actually need to be?

You do not need to buy everything at once. LiveSolv organizes preparedness by the problem you need to solve and how long you want to be able to handle it.

Level 1

Need it now

A storm is coming, the power is already out, or you have a day or two to get ready. Focus on water, food, charging, medication, lighting, weather information and free steps you can take immediately.

Level 2

Get through a few days

Build enough food, water, backup power and basic supplies to stay functional through a short disruption without scrambling for every meal or phone charge.

Level 3

Be comfortably prepared

Add more margin. Think longer food and water reserves, dependable recharging, communications, household essentials and backup plans that make a multi-day or multi-week disruption less stressful.

Level 4

Build serious resilience

For people who want more independence from fragile systems: larger energy storage, meaningful solar recharging, deeper water and food reserves, redundant communications and plans for extended disruptions.

The old survival “Rule of Threes” is useful as a priority reminder, not a stopwatch.
Air comes before shelter from dangerous exposure, shelter comes before water, and water comes before food. Actual survival limits vary enormously by conditions and the person. LiveSolv uses the idea to help prioritize what matters first, not to promise fixed timelines.

Start with the problem in front of you. If you need immediate help, use the simple storm grocery list. If you are preparing ahead, start with water, food and backup power.
