How LiveSolv works

Tell us what you're trying to solve, not which technical specification you think you need.

Prepared doesn't have to mean expensive.

If you can afford a complete backup system, we'll help you choose one. If you've got $25 and a storm coming tomorrow, we'll help you with that too.

The goal is the same: solve the problem you actually have.
