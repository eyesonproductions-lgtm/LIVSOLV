Which emergency food kit should I actually buy?

For a storm arriving tomorrow, buy ordinary shelf-stable groceries. Long-life kits make more sense when you specifically want compact food you can store for years.

Our simple decision rule

Storm in a few days: skip specialty food and use the storm grocery list.

Small long-term reserve: ReadyWise 72-hour or Mountain House 3-Day.

Building more food economically: compare current Augason Farms kits.

Food you can rotate through camping: Mountain House.

The catch: stored food can create a water problem

Many emergency meals require water. Do not buy weeks of dehydrated or freeze-dried food without increasing your water reserve to support preparation.

You can skip emergency-food kits entirely

A rotating pantry of shelf-stable food your household already eats is often cheaper and more useful. Purpose-built kits earn their space when long shelf life, compact storage and convenience matter enough to justify them.
