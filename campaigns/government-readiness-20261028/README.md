## Caption

What happens when the basics stop working?

Governments are asking households to prepare for disruptions. The timeframes differ:
• Netherlands: 72 hours without basic essentials.
• Norway and Sweden: at least one week.
• Germany: ten days if possible; even three days helps.
• U.S. CDC: at least three days of water, aiming for two weeks if possible.

These are current preparedness recommendations, not proof of a scheduled worldwide blackout. Some advice predates this week's headlines: Norway changed its recommendation in May 2024. We did not verify a new blanket three-week warning covering electricity, gas and water.

Start free: find your flashlight, charge available power banks, write down contacts, agree on check-ins, and count the safe water and food you already have. Build gradually within your budget. Never use generators, grills or outdoor fuel-burning stoves indoors.
