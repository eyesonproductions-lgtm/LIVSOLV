## Caption

The power is out. Could everyone in your house find a flashlight in 60 seconds?

Start with five things you can sort out before you need them:
1. Give a working light a permanent home.
2. Decide what actually needs backup power.
3. Write down your check-in plan. If cellular service fails, check whether Wi-Fi still works.
4. Count your safe water. CDC recommends at least 1 gallon per person per day for 3 days, building toward two weeks if possible. Plan extra for pets and additional needs.
5. Put the plan on paper so it works offline.

Start with what you own. Fill the gaps gradually. Save this and share it with your household.
