What should I keep in my car in case I get stranded?

Build for the problems that actually strand people: dead battery, low tire, no phone power, darkness, heat or cold, minor injury and waiting longer than expected for help.

The short list: jump-start capability, tire inflation, hands-free light, reflective warning gear, phone power, safe drinking water, food you will actually eat, first aid and seasonal warmth.

If the car cannot move

Your kit should help you wait safely, not turn you into a roadside mechanic.

Make yourself visible where it is safe to do so, preserve phone battery, stay aware of traffic and weather, and call appropriate roadside or emergency help.
