What should I keep in my car in case I get stranded?

Build for the boring problems first: dead battery, low tire, no phone power, darkness, heat or cold, minor injury and waiting longer than expected for help.

The short list: jump starter, tire inflator, flashlight or headlamp, reflective warning gear, phone charging, water, shelf-stable snack, basic first aid, and seasonal protection.

The two things most likely to get you moving again

1. Portable jump starter: NOCO Boost GB40

For many ordinary cars and small SUVs, this is a strong default. NOCO rates it for 12V batteries, up to 6.0L gas engines and 3.0L diesel engines. It is compact, has reverse-polarity protection and can also provide USB power in a pinch.

Do not shop by the biggest amp number on the box. Match the jump starter to your engine size.

2. Tire inflator: keep this simple

If you want inexpensive and compact, a 12V inflator that plugs into the car is hard to beat because you do not have another battery to remember to charge. Slime's 12V Digital Tire Inflator has a digital pressure setting and automatic shutoff.

Then add the cheap stuff

Visibility: reflective triangles or other warning gear.

Light: a headlamp is especially useful because it leaves both hands free.

Phone: charging cable plus a small backup battery.

Waiting: water, shelf-stable snack and a basic first-aid kit.

Weather: blanket/layers in cold climates, and sun/heat protection where heat is the bigger risk.

If the car cannot move

Your kit should help you wait safely, not turn you into a roadside mechanic.

Make yourself visible where it is safe to do so, preserve phone battery, stay aware of traffic and weather, and call appropriate roadside or emergency help. A damaged tire, dangerous shoulder or mechanical failure may still require professional help.

LivSolv rule: build the car kit for where you actually drive. A winter mountain kit and a summer South Carolina kit should not be identical. We will break those into separate short lists instead of pretending one giant bag works everywhere.
