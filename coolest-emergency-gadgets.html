THE FUN LIST

Cool emergency gadgets that are actually useful.

Not tactical junk. These are clever things that solve real problems at home, in the car or when normal systems stop working.

Our rule: A gadget makes this page only if it does something genuinely useful. You do not need everything here. Start with the problems most likely to strand you, leave you without light or communication, or make an injury harder to handle.

Car: the little things that can get you moving again

CAR FAVORITE

Fanttik X8 APEX tire inflator

A cordless inflator you can set to the pressure you need and let it stop automatically. It reaches 150 PSI, charges by USB-C, has a built-in light and can provide emergency USB power to a phone. This pairs perfectly with a lithium jump pack: one handles the battery, the other handles the tire.

CHEAP + SMART

resqme car escape tool

Tiny spring-loaded side-window breaker plus protected seatbelt cutter. Keep it reachable from the driver's seat, not buried in the trunk.

Know your glass: small impact breakers are intended for tempered side glass. Laminated side windows behave differently, so check the markings on your own vehicle.

See resqme →

Medical: genuinely clever upgrades

COOL UPGRADE

Leatherman Raptor Rescue

Real trauma shears that fold down to five inches and add a strap cutter, ring cutter, oxygen wrench and carbide glass breaker. Developed with input from emergency professionals and backed by Leatherman's 25-year warranty. Not required, but an excellent upgrade for a serious home or car medical kit.

See Raptor Rescue →
SIMPLE + BRILLIANT

SAM Splint

A lightweight foam-covered aluminum splint that stores nearly flat or rolled and becomes rigid when shaped. Useful for immobilizing an injured arm, wrist, ankle or other extremity while appropriate medical help is being reached.

Power + light

OWNED + LOVED

BLAVOR PN-W05

A rugged 10,000mAh phone battery with USB-C charging, wireless charging, built-in flashlights and an integrated solar panel. We own it, have tested it and love it for travel and backup power.

Reality check: the little solar panel is emergency trickle charging, not the normal way to recharge a 10,000mAh battery. Charge it from the wall before you need it.

LIGHT + BACKUP POWER

LuminAID PackLite Max

A collapsible solar lantern that stores flat, provides up to roughly 50 hours of light and has a small built-in battery for emergency phone charging. Particularly useful because a lantern lights a room without consuming flashlight batteries.

Communication + signaling

OFF-GRID MESSAGING

ZOLEO satellite communicator

The clever part is that you write and read messages on your phone through the ZOLEO app. The little device provides the satellite connection when cellular and Wi-Fi are unavailable, and also supports check-in and SOS functions.

Fox 40 Classic whistle

A 115 dB pealess whistle with no moving parts to freeze, jam or deteriorate. It is a tiny, inexpensive way to signal for help without exhausting yourself yelling. Put one in the car, go bag and outdoor kit.

The offline gadget nobody thinks about

WORKS WITH ZERO POWER

Pocket Ref

A pocket-sized printed reference stuffed with practical tables, conversions and technical information. The concept is almost comically low-tech, which is exactly why it belongs here: useful reference information still works when the phone, internet and power do not.

Don't forget repair

A tiny repair kit can solve more real emergency problems than another fancy gadget. Keep quality duct/repair tape, zip ties, self-fusing silicone tape, hose clamps, strong cord, a basic sewing kit, utility knife, adjustable wrench, multitool and work gloves together. Broken hoses, torn tarps, damaged clothing, loose fittings and temporary weatherproofing are ordinary emergency problems.
