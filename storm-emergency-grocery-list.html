Power may go out?

Just tell me what to get at the grocery store.

You do not need survival buckets, specialty food or a huge budget. Whether the threat is severe weather, a grid emergency, a planned shutoff or another disruption, if you have one grocery run to get ready, start here.

If you only read one thing, buy this.

For each person in your house, aim for enough to cover at least 3 days.

Water. Peanut butter. Crackers or tortillas. Canned chicken or tuna. Beans. Soup or chili. Canned or packaged fruit. Granola or protein bars. Nuts or trail mix. Shelf-stable milk. Cereal or oatmeal. A few foods everyone actually likes.

And make sure you own a manual can opener.

Before an expected outage, do these free things too.

1
Freeze containers of water.They help the freezer stay cold and become usable water after they thaw.
2
Get the refrigerator and freezer cold now.Keep the refrigerator at 40°F or below and freezer at 0°F or below.
3
Group frozen food together.A full freezer holds cold longer.
4
Freeze things you won't use beforehand.Meat, milk and leftovers can stay cold longer when frozen beforehand.
5
Charge everything.Phones, battery banks, power stations and rechargeable lights.

If the power goes out, stop opening the refrigerator.

An unopened refrigerator generally keeps food safely cold for about 4 hours. A full freezer can hold temperature for roughly 48 hours, while a half-full freezer is closer to 24 hours, if the door stays closed.

Emergency nutrition is about keeping you functional, not proving what a body can survive.

People can sometimes survive periods with far less food than an emergency plan should provide. That does not make severe restriction a good preparedness strategy. Plan enough food to preserve strength, judgment, temperature regulation and the ability to do the physical work an emergency may require.

LivSolv rule: survival thresholds and preparedness targets are not the same thing. Do not intentionally ration a household toward starvation-level intake because another person survived it.
