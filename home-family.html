Home + family

Keep the people okay when the systems are not.

A resilient household can stay lit, communicate, handle temperature problems, manage ordinary injuries and make good decisions when normal services are interrupted.

Start with the household.

You do not have to turn preparedness into a lifestyle. Cover the things your family depends on, know what you would do if they stopped working, and improve the plan over time.
