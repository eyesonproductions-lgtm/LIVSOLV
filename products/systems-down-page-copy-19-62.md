# PAGE 21 - WATER SOURCE + TREATMENT DECISION

# WHICH WATER SHOULD WE USE?
## Use the safest available source first.

### 1. STORED SAFE WATER AVAILABLE?
[ ] Yes: use sealed commercial water or properly stored household water first.
[ ] No: continue below.

### 2. KNOWN TREATED SOURCE CONFIRMED SAFE?
[ ] Yes: follow current utility and public-health instructions.
[ ] No / unsure: treat it as potentially unsafe.

### 3. BOIL-WATER OR DO-NOT-USE NOTICE?
[ ] Boil-water notice: follow the exact current instructions from the water authority.
[ ] Do-not-use notice or chemical contamination: boiling may not make water safe. Use another source and follow authorities.
[ ] No notice available: use the best verified information and the safest source you have.

### 4. UNTREATED SOURCE IS THE ONLY OPTION?
[ ] Let cloudy water settle and pre-filter particles when appropriate.
[ ] Use a treatment method suitable for the suspected hazard.
[ ] Follow the product label and current public-health guidance exactly.
[ ] Keep treated water covered and protected from recontamination.

**Important:** Clear water is not necessarily safe. Most portable filters do not remove every chemical, salt or virus. Do not guess at chemical treatment amounts.

## WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN
- **Watt-hours (Wh)** estimate stored energy and possible runtime.
- **Watts (W)** describe how much power a device needs at a moment.
- A unit may have enough energy but still be unable to start a high-surge appliance.
- Real runtime is lower than the label capacity because of conversion losses, temperature, battery condition and reserve.

# PRODUCTION NOTES FOR DESIGN REVIEW

- Keep every page readable at normal print size. Do not compress tables until handwriting space becomes unusable.
- Create clearly labeled extra-copy pages for inventories that may exceed the starter rows, especially medication, food, power loads, records, pets and expense tracking.
- Repeat urgent safety warnings where the risk occurs. Do not move all safety language into fine print.
- Keep workbook cross-references plain and optional. The printed binder must still work during a power or internet outage.
- Verify all official safety thresholds and emergency wording during final QA before sale.
- Do not insert current affiliate products or model-specific recommendations into the binder.
