## Controlling product

**LIVSOLV Systems Down Household Binder**

Current scope is approximately 60 useful binder pages plus:

- nine-tab automated Household Calculator workbook
- Start Here guide
- Quick Cards
- US Letter and A4 editions
- ink-saver editions
- First 72 Hours action sequence inside the larger system
- Two-Week Continuity Plan

The product is a calm household emergency decision system covering household dependencies, power, generator and solar planning, water, food and cold storage, cooking, medications and medical devices, heat and cold, sensory and accessibility needs, communications, home systems, records and cash, pets, vehicles, separation, stay/leave decisions, evacuation, first 72 hours, two-week continuity, and household handoff/review.
