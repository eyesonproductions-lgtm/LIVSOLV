# BINDER SECURITY NOTE
The operating binder needs to be accessible enough to use during an emergency. That can conflict with storing identity and financial records in it. Consider keeping highly sensitive originals/full account information in a protected grab file, safe or other secure location. The binder can contain a clear instruction such as “DOCUMENT FILE: HOME SAFE” rather than exposing everything to anyone who opens the binder.
