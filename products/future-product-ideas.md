# Future product ideas

## Faraday storage

- Phone option: Mission Darkness Non-Window Faraday Bag for Phones. Present it as a practical signal-isolation option for cellular, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, GPS, key-fob relay protection, and privacy. Do not promise guaranteed nuclear-EMP protection.
- Several-electronics option: Mission Darkness Faraday duffel. Intended for storing multiple small emergency electronics such as phones, radios, flashlights, power banks, drives, and compact chargers.
- Explain possible reasons without sensationalism: signal isolation, reduced tracking, compromised networks, cyberattack-related communications disruption, EMP concerns, or severe solar-weather preparedness.
- Require readers to test the closure and signal blocking when purchased and periodically afterward. Flexible shielding and closures can wear.
- Before publishing product links, verify the exact model, dimensions, lab-test claims, current price, and whether an Amazon Associates link is available under tag `livsolv-20`.
