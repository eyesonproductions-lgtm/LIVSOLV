# LivSolv Emergency Home Binder
## Live in the solution.

PRODUCT STATUS: Working master, September 2026

PURPOSE
This is not a generic preparedness checklist. It is a household operating manual for emergencies: what to do first, where critical things are, what the household has, what it lacks, and how to keep functioning when phones, internet, power, water or normal services are unavailable.

DESIGN RULES
- Emergency-first: the most time-critical pages must be usable in seconds.
- Paper-first: critical instructions cannot depend on a charged phone or internet.
- Household-specific: blanks and worksheets turn generic advice into an actual plan.
- Capability-first: HAVE IT / KNOW WHERE IT IS / KNOW HOW TO USE IT / NEED IT / $0 ALTERNATIVE / PRIORITY.
- Low cognitive load: large headings, checkboxes, short instructions, tabbed sections.
- Separate sensitive documents from the everyday reference binder where appropriate. The binder should tell the household where protected originals/copies are stored without unnecessarily exposing identity or financial data.
- Include a secure digital-backup plan for important records, but never make digital access the only copy.
- Review dates on pages that expire or change.
- Current product recommendations live on LivSolv.com so the printed binder does not become obsolete.

RESEARCH NOTES INCORPORATED
- Experienced preparedness communities repeatedly emphasize paper maps, multiple evacuation routes, household roles, departure countdowns, offline instructions, equipment procedures and keeping the binder maintained rather than treating it as a one-time checklist.
- A practical security distinction is useful: everyday emergency operating instructions can live in an accessible binder while sensitive originals/full financial records stay in a protected grab file or secure storage.
- Financial preparedness guidance supports gathering important documents, protecting records, maintaining property photos/inventory, keeping receipts after disaster and maintaining some accessible emergency cash.
