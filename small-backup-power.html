Anker Nano Power Bank A1259

10,000mAh · built-in USB-C cable · up to 30W

What is it for?

Phones and small USB devices. It is the inexpensive answer when you do not need AC outlets or to keep a laptop and router running for hours. A 10,000mAh bank is generally in the neighborhood of roughly one to two modern-phone recharges after normal conversion losses, depending heavily on the phone.

This is not a tiny power station. That is the point. If all you need is communication power, do not spend hundreds of dollars solving a bigger problem.

How we estimate runtime

Runtime is roughly usable watt-hours divided by average watts. We allow for conversion losses. Real runtime changes with device draw, temperature, battery state and whether AC conversion is required.

LoadPlanning estimate on BLUETTI 288Wh
10W routerAbout 20–24 hours
50W laptopAbout 4–5 hours
Router + 50W laptopAbout 3.5–4 hours
100W continuous loadAbout 2–2.4 hours
Planning estimates, not promises. Measure your actual device when runtime matters. Watt-hours tell you how much energy is stored. Output wattage tells you whether the station can operate the load at all.

UPS vs power station

A normal power station does not automatically provide uninterrupted switchover. BLUETTI specifies the Elite 30 V2 at ≤10ms UPS switchover. For critical medical or computer equipment, follow that equipment manufacturer's backup-power requirements.
