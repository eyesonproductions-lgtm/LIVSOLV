Small problem. Small solution.

I just want to keep my laptop and phone running.

Then do not buy a giant home-backup battery. Match the power source to the job.

Fast answer: For phones alone, start with a good USB-C power bank. For a laptop plus phones, a roughly 200–300Wh portable power station is a useful small class to compare. Add a compact solar panel only if you need to recharge away from an outlet for longer periods.

Choose by what you actually need

Phones only

A USB-C PD power bank is smaller, cheaper and easier to carry than a power station.

Laptop + phones

A small portable power station gives you more capacity and usually AC plus USB outputs.

Laptop + phones + longer outage

Add compatible solar charging. Solar is a recharge method, not extra battery capacity.

Do I need solar?

Not automatically. If you can recharge from the wall, car or another working power source, a battery may be all you need. Solar starts becoming valuable when you expect to be away from working outlets long enough to deplete the battery.

LiveSolv rule: Do not spend $1,500 to solve a $150 problem. We will cover small power banks, compact power stations and small solar combinations separately so you can stop at the level that actually solves your problem.
