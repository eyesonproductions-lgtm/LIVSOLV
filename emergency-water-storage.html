Emergency water storage

How much emergency water should I store?

Start with enough safe water for three days. If you have the room, build toward two weeks. You do not need a garage full of giant barrels to get started.

The number: Store at least 1 gallon per person per day. That means 3 gallons for one person for 3 days, 6 gallons for two people, and 12 gallons for four people. CDC recommends at least a 3-day supply and says to try for 2 weeks if possible. Add more for pets, hot weather, illness or other higher needs.

That is a baseline, not a comfort allowance. The gallon includes drinking, food preparation, brushing teeth and limited hygiene. Store more in hot climates and for pets, pregnancy, illness or other household needs.

If you have $0

Do not wait for special equipment. Before a forecast emergency, fill clean food-safe containers you already own. Use durable containers with tight lids and never reuse containers that held bleach, pesticides, fuel or other toxic chemicals.

Commercially bottled water is also a straightforward reserve. Keep it in its original sealed container and follow the manufacturer's date/storage instructions.

You can also freeze suitable water containers with room for expansion. They add cold mass to the freezer during an outage and become drinking water as they thaw.

How to store it safely

For water you fill yourself, use food-grade containers. CDC recommends labeling them as drinking water with the storage date, replacing self-filled water every 6 months, and keeping it cool, out of direct sunlight and away from gasoline, pesticides and other toxic substances.

Heavy water belongs low. Water weighs about 8.3 pounds per gallon. Avoid loading questionable shelves, and in apartments distribute larger reserves rather than concentrating enormous weight in one spot.

Storage eventually runs out

Your complete water plan has three layers: stored safe water, another water source, and a way to treat questionable water. Get the stored-water layer handled first. Then add treatment so the plan does not end when the last container is empty.
