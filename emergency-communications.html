When the phone stops being enough

How do I get information or contact people if cell service is down?

First decide whether you need to receive information, talk locally, or reach somebody beyond local infrastructure. Those are three different problems.

Fast answer: Official alerts at home? Get a NOAA alert radio with county-specific S.A.M.E. alerts. Nearby family? Use simple FRS radios. Need to reach outside the cell network? Use a satellite communicator.

Three simple choices

Just hear official alerts

Midland WR120

This is the simple home choice. It can stay on standby and use S.A.M.E. programming so alerts can be targeted to your county instead of every alert in the region.

Talk nearby without cell towers

Motorola TALKABOUT T600

FRS handheld radios require no subscription and are made for short-range direct communication. The T600 adds USB-C charging, AA-battery backup capability, NOAA weather alerts and a waterproof design.

Ignore the giant “35-mile” number. Motorola itself lists much shorter neighborhood range. Buildings, hills and trees matter.

Reach outside the cell network

Garmin inReach

A satellite communicator is the next level when local cellular infrastructure is unavailable. inReach devices can support satellite messaging and SOS with an active service plan and suitable access to the sky.

Do not buy one device expecting it to do everything. A weather radio receives information. FRS radios talk directly over a limited local distance. A satellite communicator reaches beyond local cell infrastructure. Those are different jobs.

LivSolv communication ladder

Level 1: Receive official alerts. Level 2: Communicate around your property or neighborhood. Level 3: Coordinate farther away where terrain allows. Level 4: Add satellite capability when you need a path that does not depend on local cell towers.

LivSolv rule: Do not pay for satellite service if all you need is a weather alert radio. Do not expect a pair of walkie-talkies to replace satellite communication. Buy the smallest layer that solves the actual problem.
