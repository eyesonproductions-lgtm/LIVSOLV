When the phone stops being enough

How do I get information or contact people if cell service is down?

First decide whether you need to receive information, talk locally, or reach somebody beyond local infrastructure. Those are three different problems.

Fast answer: For official alerts, start with a battery-powered NOAA Weather Radio receiver. For nearby family or a small group, consider legal two-way radio options such as FRS. For communication beyond cell coverage, satellite communicators are the next level.

Choose the problem

I only need official alerts

NOAA Weather Radio All Hazards broadcasts official weather warnings, watches, forecasts and other hazard information 24/7 from the National Weather Service. A receiver with alert capability can work independently of your phone's cellular data connection.

I need to talk to family nearby

FRS handheld radios can provide short-range, direct radio communication without relying on a cell tower. Real-world range depends heavily on terrain, buildings and obstructions, so do not treat the maximum range printed on a box as a promise.

I need to reach people when there is no cell service

A satellite communicator can provide messaging outside cellular coverage when it has suitable satellite access. Garmin's inReach line, for example, supports satellite messaging and SOS with an active service plan.

I want layers

A strong household plan can combine a weather radio for incoming alerts, local radios for nearby family, and satellite communication for situations where local infrastructure is unavailable.

Important: “Radio” does not mean unlimited range. Mountains, trees, buildings, antenna height, power and radio service all matter. We will recommend by actual use case, not giant marketing range numbers.

LiveSolv communication ladder

Level 1: Receive official alerts. Level 2: Communicate around your property or neighborhood. Level 3: Coordinate with family several miles away where conditions allow. Level 4: Add satellite capability when you need a path that does not depend on local cell towers.
